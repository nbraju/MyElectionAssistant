Home: It shows days left to polling and the votes needed to win. 
The target is 50%+1 of an assumed 80% turnout if there are one or no opponents, otherwise 40% of turnout. 
It also shows A/B/C/D vote totals, ward-by-ward coverage, a daily visit target, and a to-do checklist.
Homes: Each household is tagged A (sure), B (likely), C (undecided), or D (against). 
The candidate logs up to three visits per home, adds notes, and calls or WhatsApps from the card. 
Homes can be added in bulk by pasting a list. The app warns if a voter serial number falls outside the chosen ward’s range.
Poll day: It lists A and B homes with a “voted” tick and a live percentage. 
The hints are 30% by 9 am and 60% by 11, with phone calls and vehicles after 12.
Money: It tracks spending against the candidate’s limit and warns above 90%.
Reports: These show who still needs visits, ward gaps against target, and issues raised. Any report can be sent via WhatsApp.
More: It has a phase-wise guide and checklist, a printing cost estimator, a team directory,
WhatsApp templates, and a PIN lock, backup file and CSV export.
How it is interactive
Bilingual: Telugu and English switch with one tap, and the interface is built for large touch targets.
Pamphlet designer: A canvas builds a candidate poster from the name, photo, election symbol, slogan, three promises,
phone number, and a choice of colour themes. The poster can be printed or downloaded as a PNG.
WhatsApp tools: There are one-tap templates (greeting, poll reminder, thanks), voice dictation in Telugu or English,
and per-home or per-ward sending. On a phone it shares the design image directly.
Help mode: The “❓” button turns on tooltips, so tapping any button explains what it does.
Live updates: Totals, progress bars, and reports recalculate immediately after every action.
